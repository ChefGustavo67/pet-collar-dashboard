Questions

How does it work?

A small motion sensor in the collar measures movement. The idea is to turn that into daily activity and rest summaries, then compare each day with your pet's own usual range. Today, the sensor works on our workbench. The connection to this website is not built yet.

Does it diagnose health problems?

No. PawSense is not a medical device. It is meant to help you notice changes in activity. If you are worried about your pet, please speak to a veterinarian.

When will it be available?

We do not have a release date yet. The product is in development, and we will not guess at one.

Is my email saved if I join the waitlist?

Not in this version. This website is a static prototype with no place to store sign-ups. Saving them for real needs a backend or an external form service, and we will add one before collecting any real addresses.
