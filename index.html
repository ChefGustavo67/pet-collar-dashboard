How it works

Three steps from collar to insight, once the product is built.

  1. Fit the collar to your pet

    Adjust the strap so the sensor sits comfortably at the front of your pet's neck.

  2. Collect activity and movement data

    A small motion sensor records how your pet moves and rests through the day.

  3. Explore trends and changes

    Your pet's personal dashboard compares today with their own usual routine.

Where we are today: our prototype can measure movement and orientation on the workbench. Connecting a collar to your account and collecting data from your pet at home are still in development.

What PawSense is designed to help with

These are the goals we are building towards, not guarantees.

Understand daily activity patterns

See when your pet tends to be active and when they rest.

Explore changes over time

Look back over days and weeks to see how routines shift.

Build a picture of their usual routine

Every pet is different, so comparisons are made against your own pet.

Keep pet information organized

One place for your pet's profile and activity history.

Honest about where we are

PawSense is an early project. Here is what that means.

PawSense is designed for

  • Activity awareness: seeing your pet's usual patterns.
  • Spotting changes from your pet's own baseline.
  • Giving you something concrete to talk about with your vet.

PawSense is not

  • A medical device.
  • A way to diagnose illness or replace a veterinarian.
  • Available to buy yet. The product is in development.
